kanjitomo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Overview

kanjitomo is a Japanese kanji learning app. It is designed to work entirely offline. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Data stored on your device

All data created while using the app is stored locally on your device and never leaves it:

  • Review progress — spaced-repetition scheduling data (due dates, intervals, ease factors) for each kanji you study.
  • Personal notes — any stories, keywords, or mnemonics you write for individual kanji.
  • User-added composita — custom vocabulary words you add to kanji entries.
  • Settings — your chosen theme, brightness mode, study scope, and review preferences.

This data is stored in a local database and shared preferences on your device. It is not backed up to any server operated by us.

No analytics or tracking

kanjitomo does not include any analytics SDKs, crash reporting services, or advertising frameworks. We do not track how you use the app.

No network communication

The app does not communicate with any server. All dictionary data, example sentences, stroke order data, and the handwriting recognition model are bundled with the app. An internet connection is not required.

Text-to-speech

The app uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine to read Japanese text aloud. This is processed entirely on your device by the operating system. No audio or text is sent to external services by the app.

External links

The app contains optional links (e.g. "Buy me a coffee", bug report email, open-source project websites) that open in your device's browser or email client. Interacting with those services is subject to their own privacy policies.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We do not receive or store any payment information. Purchase receipts are validated locally on your device.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from children. Since no personal data is collected from any user, no special provisions are needed.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. You can also reset your review progress from within the app (Statistics screen).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same URL and the "last updated" date above will be revised.
