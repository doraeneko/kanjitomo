## Full description (4000 chars max)
kanjitomo helps you learn to read and write Japanese kanji through active recall — draw every character by hand, look it up instantly, and reinforce it with spaced repetition.

DRAW TO LOOK UP
Draw any kanji on the canvas and a neural network recognizes it instantly — even with messy handwriting. It covers over 6,500 characters including all Jōyō kanji, hiragana, and katakana. Tap a result to see meanings, readings, stroke order animation, compound words, and example sentences.

CAMERA LOOKUP
Photograph printed Japanese text from a textbook, sign, or menu. The app detects kanji in the image and shows them as tappable overlays. Pinch to zoom, tap the characters you need, and look them up without drawing. Works for individual kanji and compound words.

WORD LOOKUP
Build compound words character by character — draw each one or type directly — and search a dictionary of over 60,000 entries (JMdict). Find readings, meanings, and add words to your review pool.

SPACED REPETITION REVIEW
Four card types test different skills:
• Draw from meaning — shown the keyword, draw the kanji from memory
• Kanji recognition — shown the kanji, recall its reading and meaning
• Reading cloze — fill in the reading of a word in a sentence
• Draw in sentence — write the kanji in context

The SM-2 algorithm schedules reviews so you see each character right when you're about to forget it.

YOU DECIDE WHAT TO STUDY
Add kanji by JLPT level, in RTK order, or pick individual characters. Choose which compound words matter to you. Your review deck reflects what you actually need — not a one-size-fits-all curriculum.

BUILT FOR SERIOUS LEARNERS
• Personal keywords and mnemonics for every kanji
• Compound words (composita) ranked by frequency with JLPT levels
• Real example sentences with furigana and translations
• Animated stroke order for correct writing form
• Statistics tracking across all four card types
• Five color themes

COMPLETELY OFFLINE
Everything runs on your device. No internet connection, no account, no tracking. Over 6,500 characters, 60,000+ dictionary entries, and 6,000+ example sentences — all bundled in the app.

WORKS WITH YOUR TEXTBOOK
Use kanjitomo alongside any JLPT workbook or kanji study book. Photograph a page to look up unfamiliar characters, add them to your review pool, and let spaced repetition reinforce what you study on paper.
